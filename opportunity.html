Opportunity Ranking

Where is non-pharm × drug research effort most under-allocated relative to what's already been found? opportunity_score = predicted_uplift × gap_weight — cell lines with a promising real signal but almost no direct testing rank highest; already well-studied lines are suppressed even if their signal is strong.

This is a different question from the Candidates worklist: that page ranks individual drug × modifier × cell-line rows by an additive, five-signal priority_score. This page ranks whole cell lines (and cancer types) by a multiplicative two-factor formula — a cell line can only rank highly here if it has both a real synergy signal and a real evidence gap; neither factor can compensate for the other being weak.
Methodology

predicted_uplift (0–1): the fraction of a cell line's genuinely-tested (Tier 1 direct or Tier 2 inferred) interactions that are synergistic. Not a magnitude estimate — a Thermal Enhancement Ratio and an IC50 fold-shift aren't comparable on one scale without real work to make them so, so this counts real synergistic calls, not blended numbers.

gap_weight = 1 / (1 + log(1 + tier_1_direct_count)): a cell line with zero Tier 1 rows gets the maximum gap_weight (1.0) regardless of how much Tier 2/2c/3/4 evidence exists around it; a well-tested line (many real Tier 1 rows) is smoothly suppressed, never to exactly zero.

Cancer-type rows roll up their constituent cell lines' scores (mean and max), and are flagged low-confidence when backed by only one cell line — shown, not hidden.
