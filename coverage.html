Coverage Gap Matrix

Most resources show what's known; this shows what's missing. Each cell is a (drug × modifier type) pair for a drug with at least one documented resistance mechanism. gap_score = mechanism-annotation richness ÷ (1 + existing interaction count) — a drug with well-understood resistance biology and almost nothing tested against it in that modifier class is a genuine, citable gap, not just an empty cell.

Darker = bigger gap (more resistance-mechanism understanding relative to how little has actually been tested). A light/empty-looking cell is not proof of "nothing to find" — it means little has been curated here yet either way.
