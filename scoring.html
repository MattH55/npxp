Synergy Scoring

Prediction Layer, Step 1: classical synergy quantification (Bliss, HSA, Loewe, ZIP) for a modifier-level × drug-dose viability matrix — e.g. BHB concentration × drug dose, or temperature × drug dose. Scores are effect excess: positive = synergistic, negative = antagonistic. Results are computed on demand and never written into the database.

Models:
Loewe/ZIP fit Hill curves to the monotherapy edges (≥3 dose points each); ZIP is the delta score over fitted surfaces, not SynergyFinder's exact curve-shift correction.
