NPxP — Non-Pharm × Pharm

An open, evidence-graded database modeling interactions between non-pharmacological interventions — dietary/metabolic, thermal, hypoxic, and similar modifiers — and pharmacological agents, at the cellular level, across cancer cell lines. Every claim traces back to a cited source and an explicit evidence tier.
